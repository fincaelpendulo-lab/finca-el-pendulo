Kinds of retreat

We host every kind of retreat

Our team works closely with you to create unforgettable experiences for your guests. Some of the ones we host most often:

Holistic and healing retreats

Ceremonies in the Osho Hut and the Oráculo, temazcal, cacao ceremony and fire circles. Nature and quiet for work on body, mind and spirit.

Yoga and meditation retreats

Sunrise practice facing the agave fields, spacious rooms for yoga, meditation and breathwork, with mats and blankets set up if your group needs them.

Company retreats and team building

The Ágora Gnosis hall for meetings, presentations and planning, with lodging and meals on site. Between sessions: horses, bonfires, the lake and workshops that bring the team together.

Leadership and personal growth retreats

Spaces for circles, group work and personal process, far from city noise. Nature and the pace of the Finca help people reflect, decide and return with clarity.

Creative and art retreats

An art studio with five pottery wheels, long tables for group work and corners to write, paint or create outdoors.

Wellbeing and digital detox retreats

A pool, spa, gym with pilates reformer, food made with what grows in the greenhouse and trails around the lake for real rest.
