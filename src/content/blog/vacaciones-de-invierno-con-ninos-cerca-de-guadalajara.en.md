Looking for things to do with kids near Guadalajara over the winter break? You have two kinds of options: the city favourites (Los Colomos forest, the zoo, the JAPI museum, KidZania) or a day in the countryside, just over an hour away in Los Altos de Jalisco. At the Finca, a Day Pass costs $400 MXN per adult, half price for children under 11 and free for children under 5. It includes a heated pool, a jacuzzi, the common areas and a horse ride booked in advance.

Below: why a day outdoors is worth the drive, an hour-by-hour plan, how to keep children safe around horses and water, and what to pack.

## Why spend a day in the countryside

In Mexico, the public school winter break runs from [21 December 2026 to 5 January 2027](https://www.infobae.com/mexico/2026/07/16/vacaciones-de-invierno-semana-santa-y-mas-las-fechas-del-ciclo-escolar-sep-2026-2027/). That is two long weeks, and without a plan they drift towards the sofa and a screen.

The World Health Organization advises that children aged 3 and 4 spend [no more than one hour a day of sedentary screen time](https://www.who.int/news/item/24-04-2019-to-grow-up-healthy-children-need-to-sit-less-and-play-more), "less is better", and get at least three hours of physical activity spread through the day. A day outside covers that without trying.

In 2018 the American Academy of Pediatrics published a [clinical report on the power of play](https://doi.org/10.1542/peds.2018-2058). Its message fits in a line: play is not frivolous. It builds attention and self-control, and playing with a parent helps children regulate stress.

What about nature itself? A [2018 systematic review](https://doi.org/10.1136/jech-2018-210436) pulled together 35 studies of children and teenagers. About half of the findings showed a positive link between time in nature and mental health; almost half found no significant difference. The authors call for more rigorous studies, so read it as a promising sign rather than a promise.

Writer Richard Louv gave the gap a name in [*Last Child in the Woods*](https://www.hachettebookgroup.com/titles/richard-louv/last-child-in-the-woods/9781565126053/) (2005): "nature-deficit disorder". He uses it to describe what children lose as their bond with the natural world weakens.

## Things to do with kids near Guadalajara: in the city and an hour out

The city has plenty for families. A local [list of 20 places for children in Guadalajara](https://www.tusbuenasnoticias.com/noticias/turismo/2026/01/16/55773-estos-son-los-mejores-20-lugares-para-los-pequenos-en-guadalajara) includes Bosque Los Colomos, the Guadalajara Zoo, the Michin Aquarium, KidZania, Selva Mágica and the JAPI museum, which is the [renovated former Trompo Mágico](https://www.tusbuenasnoticias.com/amp/noticias/turismo/2024/10/12/37939-jalisco-paseo-interactivo-donde-esta-ubicado). Nearly all of them are inside the metro area.

What those lists leave out are countryside days close by. Here is how the two compare:

| | In the city | An hour out, in Los Altos |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Getting there | Short, within the metro area | Just over an hour by highway |
| Kind of day | Museums, zoo, theme parks | Countryside, animals, pool and walks |
| Pace | Several stops in one day | One place, at your own pace |
| Best for | A morning or an afternoon | A full day |

They work well together: one day of the break for the city, another for the countryside.

## A day at the Finca with kids, hour by hour

The Day Pass runs from 10:00 am to 7:00 pm. Here is one way to spend it; adjust it to your children's ages and energy.

- **8:30 am.** Leave Guadalajara. Leave earlier if you plan to stop for breakfast in Tepatitlán.
- **10:00 am.** Arrive at the Finca. Start with the [stables](/en/spaces): nine horses live here, and five of them were born on the Finca.
- **11:00 am.** Horse ride, booked ahead of time.
- **12:30 pm.** Walk to Lake Ávalon, home to koi and turtles.
- **1:30 pm.** Pool time. The pool is heated every day of the year, so December is no problem.
- **3:00 pm.** Lunch. You are welcome to bring your own food: there are grills you can use, and the Finca can keep your food refrigerated while you enjoy the day.
- **4:30 pm.** Quiet time: the jacuzzi for the grown-ups, free play in the garden for the kids.
- **6:00 pm.** Sunset on the lakeside paths, then head home at 7:00 pm.

With a Day Pass you can also book any of the [Finca's experiences](/en/experiences): horse care (brushing, feeding and spending time with the horses), children's activities, piñata making, or time with Fifi and Vico, two wolves rescued in 2019, guided by the team. Ask for them on WhatsApp when you book your Day Pass.

One family who spent a weekend here put it this way in a Google review: "time close to the Finca's animals and warm, friendly care from every member of the team."

## Horses and kids: how to ride safely

The horse ride is included in the Day Pass if you book it in advance. Our team teaches people of every age how to care for and handle the horses. Even so, it helps to arrive with a few rules in mind.

The American Academy of Pediatrics sums up the essentials in its guide to [horseback riding safety for children](https://healthychildren.org/English/healthy-living/sports/Pages/Horseback-Riding.aspx):

1. **A helmet every time.** The Finca lends helmets; tell us your child's age when you book so we have the right size.
2. **Always supervised.** Children should not ride alone or on horses of unknown temperament.
3. **Sturdy footwear.** Closed boots or trainers with a small heel that cover the ankle. No sandals.
4. **Never walk behind a horse.** Avoid shouting and sudden movements near it too.
5. **Tack checked first.** Someone experienced adjusts the saddle, stirrups and reins before anyone mounts.

The same guide notes that injury risk is highest for the least experienced riders. That is why a child's first ride is best taken at a walk, with someone alongside. There is no minimum age to ride at the Finca.

## Pool and jacuzzi with kids

A heated pool does not change the basics: water needs the same attention in any season.

- **Within arm's reach.** With young children, the American Academy of Pediatrics recommends [touch supervision](https://www.healthychildren.org/English/safety-prevention/at-play/Pages/Water-Safety-And-Young-Children.aspx): an adult always close enough to touch them.
- **A water watcher, in shifts.** If several families come together, one adult watches the water with no phone and no chatting, and you swap every 15 to 20 minutes.
- **No inflatables as safety gear.** Floaties and water wings can deflate. If your child cannot swim well, use a life jacket.
- **Go easy in the jacuzzi.** A health guide from the government of British Columbia advises [talking to your doctor before children under 5 use a hot tub](https://www.healthlinkbc.ca/healthwise/child-safety-drowning-prevention-pools-and-hot-tubs) and never leaving a child alone in one.

Pool hours are 9:00 am to 8:00 pm, and glass containers are not allowed in the pool area.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much is the Day Pass for children?**
Children under 11 pay half, $200 MXN, and children under 5 come in free. Adults pay $400 MXN. It includes the pool, the jacuzzi, the common areas and a horse ride booked in advance.

**Is the pool heated in December?**
Yes. The Finca pool is heated every day of the year. Pool hours are 9:00 am to 8:00 pm.

**Is there a minimum age for the horse ride?**
No, there is no minimum age at the Finca. Rides are booked in advance, and children ride in closed shoes with a helmet the Finca lends them. A child's first ride is taken at a walk, with someone alongside.

**Is the Day Pass open on Christmas and New Year?**
Yes. The Day Pass is open on 24, 25 and 31 December and on 1 January. Book it on WhatsApp, as you would any other day.

**Can we stay the night?**
Yes. The Cabaña Burra sleeps up to 7, with double beds and bunks, and there are more options on our [stay](/en/stay) page. That way the kids get two days of pool and lake.

Tell us the date, how many adults and children are coming and whether you want a horse ride. [Book your Day Pass on WhatsApp](/en/day-pass).
