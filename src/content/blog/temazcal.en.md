The temazcal is a steam bath that dates back to pre-Hispanic Mexico. Its name comes from the Nahuatl *temazcalli*, "house of steam". Inside a small dome, stones heated in a fire are placed at the centre and sprinkled with water and herbal infusions. The steam fills the space and the session moves through stages, with songs, silence or intentions.

## What it feels like

The heat rises slowly. Many people describe it as a mix of effort and rest: the body sweats, the breath slows and the mind grows quiet. Stepping out into fresh air or cold water afterwards leaves a feeling of lightness.

## How to prepare

- Eat lightly a few hours before.
- Drink water before and after.
- Bring a swimsuit or comfortable cotton clothes and a towel.
- If you have a health condition, are pregnant or have high blood pressure, check with your doctor first.
- You can step out of the temazcal at any moment if you need to.
