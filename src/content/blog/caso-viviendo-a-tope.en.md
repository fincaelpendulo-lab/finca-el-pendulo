**Mindfulness in Everyday Life** is a yoga and meditation retreat by [Viviendo a Tope](https://viviendoatope.com/), the project of Juan Cruz and Antonio León. It has run eight times, and its latest edition took place at Finca El Péndulo, in Tepatitlán de Morelos, one hour from Guadalajara, with **45 participants**.

## Who Viviendo a Tope are

Viviendo a Tope shares yoga, meditation and wellbeing "for everyone": classes, guided practices and retreats designed so anyone, with or without experience, can bring mindfulness into daily life. Juan has practised yoga since 2012, trained as a teacher and is registered with Yoga Alliance. Together with Antonio León he leads the Mindfulness in Everyday Life retreat.

## The retreat

- **Type:** yoga and meditation.
- **Participants:** 45 in the latest edition.
- **Editions:** eight in total, the latest at the Finca.

The idea is simple and deep at once: to practise mindfulness not only on the mat but in everyday life. That is why the venue matters. A group of 45 needs large spaces to practise together, quiet corners to meditate, lodging close to everything and meals on time, so the facilitators can focus on guiding.

## Why they chose the Finca

At the Finca, the group had lodging, three meals a day, a coffee break and use of the spaces across six hectares of blue agave, gardens and Lake Ávalon. This is what they valued most:

> "The whole staff has a true spirit of service, and the facilities are excellent and very well kept."

And why they would recommend the Finca to other facilitators:

> "The organization is excellent and the staff is wonderful."

## What we learned from this retreat

- **Logistics free the facilitator.** With meals, room setup and lodging taken care of, Juan and Antonio could give their full attention to the group.
- **A large group can still feel intimate.** The Finca's spaces let everyone practise together and also break into small groups or silence.
- **Care shows.** What they highlighted most was not a particular space but how the team treated them.
