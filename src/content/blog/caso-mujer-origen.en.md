**Mujer Origen** is a retreat for women by [Franciska Decodifica](https://www.franciska-decodifica.com/) on feminine magic, sensuality, lineage healing and trauma. She has run it thirteen times, and one of its editions took place at Finca El Péndulo, in Tepatitlán de Morelos, one hour from Guadalajara, with **15 women**.

## Who Franciska Decodifica is

Franciska is a certified biodecoding therapist with more than 20 years on the path of wellbeing and healing. She has trained in ancestral work, neuropsychobiology and metamedicine, and helps women understand the emotional roots of what they live through so they can drive their own healing. Besides her retreats, she is a writer and speaker and shares her work on social media.

## The retreat

- **Type:** retreat for women: feminine magic, sensuality, lineage healing and trauma.
- **Participants:** 15 in its edition at the Finca.
- **Editions:** thirteen in total, one at the Finca.

Mujer Origen is described as "a return to yourself": days to heal women's stories, reconnect with feminine energy and use dance, guided meditation and ritual as medicine for the body. Work this deep needs a place that holds it: beautiful, quiet and with different spaces for each moment of the process.

## Why she chose the Finca

This is what Franciska liked most about holding her retreat with us:

- **The beauty and energy of the hall** where she worked with the group.
- **The staff's service**, always having everything ready to work in the spaces.
- **The variety of spaces** for each activity, from the hall to the gardens and the temazcal.
- **The meals**, nourishing and delicious.

And why she would recommend the Finca:

> "It is a place of great beauty, peace and harmony, which is essential for retreats of personal work and growth."

## What we learned from this retreat

- **The space holds the process too.** On a healing retreat, the beauty and calm of the place help each woman open up.
- **Each activity needs its own place.** Dance, circle, ritual and rest work better when they don't share one room.
- **Food is care too.** Eating well gives energy for the day's emotional work.
