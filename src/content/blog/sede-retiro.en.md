Choosing the venue is one of the decisions that shapes a retreat the most. These are the questions worth asking before you book.

## 1. Do the spaces suit what you will do?

A yoga retreat needs a firm floor and shade; a meditation retreat needs quiet; group work needs an indoor hall with good light. Ask which spaces are included, for how many hours a day and whether other groups will be there at the same time.

## 2. How will people get there?

A venue near Guadalajara makes arrival easy from the airport and from other cities. Check the route, the travel time and parking.

## 3. What does the package include?

See whether the price covers lodging, meals, coffee breaks and use of the spaces, and what costs extra. Ask about special diets.

## 4. Is there nature around?

The setting holds the process: walking among agave, sitting by a lake or spending an afternoon with horses helps people take in what happens in the sessions.

## 5. What activities can you add?

Temazcal, yoga, horse riding, workshops or fire circles enrich the programme without you having to organise them.
