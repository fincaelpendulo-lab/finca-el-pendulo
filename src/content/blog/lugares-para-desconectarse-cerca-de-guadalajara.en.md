The best weekend getaways near Guadalajara don't need a flight or a beach. Within reach you have three landscapes: the pine forests of the sierra, the shore of Lake Chapala and the farmland of Los Altos de Jalisco, just over an hour away. What matters more than the map is what you find on arrival: quiet, nature outside your door and a good reason to put your phone away.

Here is what to look for, how each kind of trip compares and what a weekend at the Finca in Tepatitlán looks like.

## How to choose weekend getaways near Guadalajara that actually rest you

Resting is not the same as changing cities. If the weekend fills up with queues, traffic and screens, you come home just as tired. Before you book, check four things.

- **Real quiet.** Crickets at night, not engines.
- **Nature without a drive.** You can walk out of your room and onto a trail.
- **Something to do with your body.** A hike, a swim, a horse ride. Moving helps the mind let go.
- **Few reasons to check your phone.** A place that pulls you outdoors.

Research backs these up, with caveats. A [2015 Stanford study](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1510459112) followed 38 healthy adults: those who walked for 90 minutes in a green area reported less rumination (the loop of going over the same thoughts) than those who walked through the city. It is a small study, and the authors themselves called for more research.

A larger survey of nearly 20,000 adults in England found that [people who spent at least 120 minutes a week in nature](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41598-019-44097-3) reported better health and wellbeing. It made no difference whether that came in one long outing or several short ones. The study is observational: it shows an association, not cause and effect.

A [2018 review from the University of East Anglia](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.envres.2018.06.030), covering 143 studies, linked more time in green space with lower salivary cortisol and a lower heart rate. The authors warn that many of those studies were of limited quality. Journalist Florence Williams followed this line of research through Korea, Finland and California for her book [The Nature Fix](https://www.florencewilliams.com/the-nature-fix) (2017).

As for the phone, a [2025 trial in PNAS Nexus](https://doi.org/10.1093/pnasnexus/pgaf017) blocked mobile internet for hundreds of participants for two weeks. Their attention and wellbeing improved, and they spent more time with other people, exercising and outdoors. Only some participants fully stuck to the block, so the result is promising rather than final.

## Forest, lake or farmland: three ways to unplug near Guadalajara

Each landscape rests you in a different way. Use this table to choose.

| Getaway | Examples | What you find | Keep in mind |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Forest | Tapalpa, Mazamitla | Pine and oak, cabins, crisp air | Pack warm layers: Tapalpa's nights are cool and frost lasts until May |
| Lake | Chapala, Ajijic | Mexico's largest natural lake, piers, sunsets | Lively lakeside towns with plenty going on |
| Farmland | Los Altos de Jalisco | Agave, red earth, ranches and open sky | Less known; arrive with a plan |

### The forest

[México Desconocido](https://www.mexicodesconocido.com.mx/tapalpa-jalisco.html) places Tapalpa among oak and fir forests, with heavy rain and mist in summer. There are walks to Las Piedrotas and the Salto del Nogal waterfall. Mazamitla, according to the same magazine's [guide to Jalisco's Pueblos Mágicos](https://www.mexicodesconocido.com.mx/pueblos-magicos-para-visitar-por-jalisco.html), is surrounded by forest. These are the classic cabin-and-fireplace trips.

### The lake

[Lake Chapala](https://www.mexicodesconocido.com.mx/lago-de-chapala-el-mas-grande-de-mexico.html) is Mexico's largest natural body of water, at around 114,659 hectares. Ajijic, on its shore, is a starting point for boat rides and for watching the sunset from the piers.

### The farmland

This is the option that rarely makes the lists. More on it below.

## Los Altos de Jalisco: the quiet option few people consider

Los Altos, the Jalisco highlands, lie east of Guadalajara. The official [Visit Mexico](https://visitmexico.com/en/itinerario/51/un-dia-en-tepatitlan-de-morelos) guide calls Tepatitlán "the Pearl of Los Altos de Jalisco", just over an hour's drive from the city, and describes its red-soil landscapes and agave fields.

For rest, that landscape has an advantage: it is open and quiet. There is a lot of sky, a long horizon and few distractions. You don't come to browse a street of shops; you come to slow down.

The Finca sits there, on six hectares on the edge of Tepatitlán. Guests say it in their own words on Google: "An incredible place to disconnect and enjoy nature." Another review mentions "a beautiful energy, lovely landscapes, time close to the Finca's animals and warm, friendly care from every member of the team."

## A weekend at the Finca, Friday to Sunday

Here is how a quiet weekend in the Jalisco highlands can look. Adjust the pace to what your body asks for.

**Friday afternoon.** Drop your bag and head out for a walk. The paths around Lake Ávalon are made for sunset, when the sky turns pink and orange. Koi and turtles live in the lake.

**Saturday morning.** If you stay in the Agaves rooms, you get the widest view over the agave fields and the paddock where the horses graze, and the best spot for sunrise. Then an open-air yoga class or a horse ride: nine horses live at the Finca, and five of them were born here.

**Saturday afternoon.** A pool heated all year, the jacuzzi or a massage at the spa. If you want something deeper, try the temazcal, a Mesoamerican steam bath with cold tubs outside for afterwards. We explain it in [what is a temazcal](/en/blog/what-is-a-temazcal). If you are pregnant, have high blood pressure or a heart condition, check with your doctor first.

**Sunday.** A hike up the Cerro de la Campana to look over the valley and the agave fields. Or a guided, respectful visit with Fifi and Vico, two black wolves rescued in 2019 who live at the Finca.

All of these are on our [Experiences](/en/experiences) page, and the places where they happen are under [Spaces](/en/spaces). Experiences are booked in advance, so write to us before you arrive and we'll help you plan.

## Getting there and when to go

The Finca is at Camino al Saucillo 861, Tepatitlán de Morelos. From Guadalajara it is just over an hour by car. If you'd rather not drive, we offer round-trip transport from Guadalajara airport or anywhere in the city, at an extra cost.

When to go:

1. **Midweek if you can.** Any destination is calmer outside long weekends.
2. **Arrive in daylight.** You'll see the road and catch the sunset at the lake.
3. **Book experiences ahead.** Horse rides, the temazcal and massages need advance booking.
4. **Bring a warm layer** for the evening and comfortable shoes for walking.

## Frequently asked questions

**What are some quiet places near Guadalajara that aren't the beach?**
You have three landscapes: the forest around Tapalpa or Mazamitla, the shore of Lake Chapala and the farmland of Los Altos de Jalisco. Choose by what you need: cold air and a cabin, water and sunsets, or open sky and silence.

**Can I go to the Finca on my own?**
